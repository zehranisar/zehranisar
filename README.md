## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏔️ Majestic North Tours
**Tourism website for Gilgit-Baltistan.** Secure authentication, online tour bookings, reviews and an admin dashboard.<br>
`React` `TypeScript` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `JWT`<br>
[🔗 Live demo](https://majestic-north-tours.vercel.app) · [📂 Repository](https://github.com/zehranisar/Majestic-North-Tours)

### ❤️ CardioScan.ai
**Heart disease risk assessment with explainable ML**, OCR lab-report reading and a health assistant.<br>
`React` `TypeScript` `FastAPI` `scikit-learn` `Supabase` `Gemini API`<br>
[🔗 Live demo](https://cardio-scan-ai-nine.vercel.app) · [📂 Repository](https://github.com/zehranisar/CardioScan.ai)

### ⚡ PowerPilot AI
**Electricity demand forecasting platform** comparing Linear Regression, Decision Tree, KNN and Random Forest models with interactive analytics.<br>
`React` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `scikit-learn` `Tailwind CSS`<br>
[🔗 Live demo](https://power-pilot-ai.vercel.app) · [📂 Repository](https://github.com/zehranisar/PowerPilot-AI)

### 🚦 Smart Traffic Signal Control (Reinforcement Learning)
**Q-learning, DQN and PPO agents** that learn to control a four-way intersection, compared with fixed-timer and longest-queue controllers over 30 unseen scenarios per traffic level. In simulation, DQN cut the average queue by about **80% in normal traffic** and **58% in rush traffic** against a 30 s fixed timer. Includes a live side-by-side demo.<br>
`Python` `Gymnasium` `Stable-Baselines3` `FastAPI` `React`<br>
[📂 Repository](https://github.com/zehranisar/smart-traffic-rl)

### 💰 NexSpend
**Expense management web app** with OCR receipt extraction, budgets, CSV export and a dashboard.<br>
`React` `Flask` `SQLite` `OCR`<br>
[🔗 Live demo](https://nex-spend.vercel.app) · [📂 Repository](https://github.com/zehranisar/NexSpend)
